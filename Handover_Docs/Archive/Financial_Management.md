[]{#_Toc237256193 .anchor}**Introduction**

This document explains the financial and accounting topics that must be
understood before software requirements are written. For each topic, it
describes what the process is, why it matters, how it normally works in
practice, the main data or documents involved, and a practical example.

|                                                                       |
| This document includes the current Jordanian tax, social-security,    |
| and other statutory rates relevant to the financial processes         |
| discussed. These figures are provided as a business reference based   |
| on the applicable rules at the time of this research. As rates,       |
| exemptions, thresholds, and regulations may change, they should be    |
| verified against the latest official Jordanian regulations before     |
| being applied. Company-specific approval limits and internal policies |
| should also be confirmed during the Requirements phase.               |

**15.1 Cash Flow Reports**

A cash-flow report shows actual cash inflows and outflows for a selected
period and the movement from opening to closing cash position. It should
be based on reliable cash-account activity and allow important totals to
be traced to underlying transactions.

**15.2 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

KPIs summarize agreed financial performance measures such as
profitability, liquidity or collection efficiency. The important
requirement at the research stage is that every KPI must have an agreed
definition, formula, data source and reporting frequency so different
users calculate the same metric consistently.

**15.3 Break-even Analysis**

Break-even analysis identifies the sales volume at which total revenue
equals total costs. It depends on fixed costs, selling price and
variable cost per unit. For example, fixed costs of JOD 8,000, selling
price JOD 25 and variable cost JOD 8 give a JOD 17 contribution per unit
and a break-even point of approximately 471 units.

**15.4 Custom Reports**

Custom reporting lets authorized users answer business-specific
questions by selecting permitted fields, periods, filters, grouping and
sorting. Saved definitions may be reused, but access must still respect
the user\'s data permissions.

**17.1 General Sales Tax (GST) - ضريبة المبيعات**

Jordan applies a standard General Sales Tax rate of 16% to goods and
services not subject to a reduced, zero-rated, exempt, or special-tax
classification. The Income and Sales Tax Department also publishes
official classifications for 0%, 2%, 4%, 5%, and 10%, as well as
exemptions and special tax.

+-------------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| **Tax treatment**                   | **Baseline rate / treatment**        |
+=====================================+======================================+
| Standard taxable goods and services | 16%                                  |
+-------------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| Reduced-rate categories             | 10%, 5%, 4%, or 2% according to the  |
|                                     | official classification              |
+-------------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| Zero-rated categories               | 0%                                   |
+-------------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| Exempt goods/services               | Exempt - no GST charged under the    |
|                                     | applicable exemption                 |
+-------------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| Special-tax goods/services          | Special rate or amount according to  |
|                                     | the official special-tax table       |
+-------------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| **PRODUCT CLASSIFICATION RULE** The system should not assume a broad       |

System impact: each product or service should have a tax category,

**17.6 Employee / Natural-Person Income Tax - ضريبة دخل الموظفين
والأفراد**

Employee income tax is not one flat payroll percentage. After applying
the relevant personal/family exemptions and taxable-income rules,
progressive brackets apply to the taxable income of a natural person.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Taxable-income band after applicable exemptions**  **Rate**
  ---------------------------------------------------- ------------------
  First JOD 5,000                                      5%

  Next JOD 5,000                                       10%

  Next JOD 5,000                                       15%

  Next JOD 5,000                                       20%

  Amount above JOD 20,000 up to JOD 1,000,000          25%

  Taxable income above JOD 1,000,000                   30%
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Current law also provides personal/family and qualifying-expense
exemptions that affect the taxable base. Those details depend on the
taxpayer\'s circumstances and should be represented as configurable
payroll/tax inputs rather than one universal deduction.

Typical payroll sequence: Gross earnings -\> social-security calculation
-\> applicable exemptions/taxable basis -\> income-tax withholding -\>
other authorized deductions -\> net salary.

# How the Topics Connect

The topics are interconnected. Payroll uses deduction and
social-security rules. Fixed expenses feed budgets, cash-flow forecasts
and break-even analysis. Expense and fixed-asset data support tax
preparation and reporting. Reconciliation improves the reliability of
balances used by reports. Internal controls and the written financial
policy govern approvals, access and accountability across all processes.
